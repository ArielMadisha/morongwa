GUIDELINE 3.1.1 — Payments / In-App Purchase (tips)
Optional tips associated with digital content have been REMOVED from the iOS app:
• Creator Donate / coffee-cup control on QwertyTV and Wall posts
• “Buy me Coffee” and wallet-donate modal for creators
• School-account Donate button

Digital content and creator services are not sold via wallet on iOS. We did not implement StoreKit IAP in this pass; we removed the non-IAP tip flows instead.

Wallet Scan QR “Send money” is person-to-person funds transfer (Guideline 3.1.1(b)), not a tip for digital content. QwertyHub checkout is for physical goods (marketplace products, food, groceries) with prepaid delivery — real-world goods, not digital content.
